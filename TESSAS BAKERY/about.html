MEET TESSA

Baking With Passion

Tessa's Baking Business started from Tessa's passion for baking cakes, cupcakes and biscuits for family and friends.

What began as something she enjoyed doing at home gradually grew into a small home bakery.

Today, Tessa focuses on creating homemade baked goods for birthdays, celebrations and other special occasions.
